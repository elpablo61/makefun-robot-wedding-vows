A [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kreutzx-one-line-robot-wedding-vows-a154ba4b) entry for the **One-Line Robot Wedding Vows** bounty ($2).

The program writes a single line of wedding vows for a robot couple to `/output/entry.txt` — one line, at most 160 characters, built to land as a joke: real vow form ("I take you...", "to have and to hold"), machine-specific detail (co-pilot, low battery, factory reset), and the punchline on the last words.

> I take you to be my lawfully wedded co-pilot, to have and to hold, in sickness and in low battery, till factory reset do us part.

## How it was chosen

Five candidate vows were written first (comedy-writer: specificity is the engine, punchline last), then pairwise-judged twice per pair with positions swapped against the judge brief's rubric — vow form, laugh in the line itself, wedding promise, machine specificity, rhythm aloud — and the winner above was submitted after a humanizer pass.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts   # writes /output/entry.txt
```
